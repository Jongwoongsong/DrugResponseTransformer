Supplementary Figure Sx. Distribution of within-structure Pearson correlation coefficients across the 41 canonical-SMILES structures held out from downstream GDSC IC50 supervision. Each point represents the correlation between observed and predicted LN_IC50 values across cell lines for one held-out structure; boxes summarize the interquartile range and median, and diamonds indicate the mean. For DRT scratch and pretrained models, structure-specific PCC values were averaged across optimization seeds 42, 123, and 2026 before visualization so that each molecular structure contributed one value per model. Classical and CSG2A comparator distributions correspond to their validation-selected final runs. The plot complements pooled metrics by showing heterogeneity in within-compound cell-line ranking performance.
